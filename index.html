Our Story

A Table Set for Steak Lovers

PRIME HOUSE was created around a simple idea: great steak deserves patience, good ingredients and a room where you can enjoy it without hurry. Our kitchen focuses on carefully selected cuts, honest seasoning and precise cooking.

Each cut is seasoned simply and finished over an open flame or in a hot cast-iron pan, depending on the cut, then rested before serving so the juices stay where they belong.

  • Ribeye & T-Bone Rich marbling, bold flavor
  • Filet & Tenderloin Lean, tender, delicate
  • Open-flame grilling Charred outside, juicy inside
